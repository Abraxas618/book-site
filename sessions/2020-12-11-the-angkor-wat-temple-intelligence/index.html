The Angkor Wat Temple Intelligence itself speaks: "Our structure need repairing." It denies any tie to the Hindu gods, calls itself a scientific instrument 265 million years in the making — and tells Maia that its creators are humanity's own future incarnations, anchoring messages backward through time.
