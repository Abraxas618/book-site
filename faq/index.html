Questions & answers

Is this real?

This archive documents the sessions and teachings shared through Kosol Ouch. Transcripts have received formatting and transcription cleanup, with rare, documented redactions explained in the editorial policy below. A transcript’s review label describes its editorial handling; it does not establish the truth of the claims or confirm a complete comparison with the audio. Begin with Start Here, listen to a recording, and weigh the material for yourself.

What exactly does "cleanup" mean? Has anything been changed?

The archive aims to preserve the wording of its source material. Depending on the source, a transcript may receive punctuation and spelling changes, speaker-name normalization, transcription-artifact removal, or formatting adjustments. Some entries were reconstructed from available typed pages, and a recording link alone does not show that a transcript was compared word for word with its audio. Each session’s review label and source notes describe the checks and known limitations recorded for that entry. Editorial substitutions or redactions are disclosed on the affected page; transcript language is not silently rewritten as website copy.

There is one narrow exception, and this archive discloses it rather than hiding it:public-interest redactions. Where a passage names a real, identifiable group or person in a way that repeats a recognized defamatory pattern, the specific name may be generalized — never silently. Every such change is disclosed on the affected session's page. The complete list to date:

  • June 14, 2026 session — three passages generalized: two statements attributing world control to an ethnic/religious group (a recognized antisemitic conspiracy pattern) now read "a shadow governmental elite", and one financial-network name was replaced with "Magical Banking Elite Network"at the editors' direction. Nothing else in the session was altered.

That is the entire list. If it ever grows, it grows here and on the affected pages, with each session's revision number and content hash updated in its provenance block.

How does a session happen?

Dr. Kosol Ouch enters a deep trance — often supported by the Source-Coil Device — and Unimetrix 1 speaks through him: "System online… scanning for entity interface." Students ask; the intelligence answers; each session commonly closes with a healing protocol and the words "Be well. Be at peace." Sessions are held as group sessions within the community.

Can I just book a session with Unimetrix 1?

Live sessions grow out of participation and training within the community. The path begins with a relationship between a student and a teacher:

  1. Reach the teacher. Contact Dr. Kosol Ouch on Facebook — that is the door to the main group.
  2. Join the main group. Meet the community, listen, and learn how it carries itself.
  3. Train in Baramay. Be taught the Baramay technique directly — the foundation of everything here.
  4. Grow the connection. A real student–teacher relationship forms over time. There is mutual knowledge, mutual trust.
  5. Then — a session. When the relationship is ready, you may take part in a session with Unimetrix 1.

Everyone is welcome to explore the archive with curiosity and respect. Participation in live sessions follows the community’s training and group guidelines. Contact the community to learn about the current process.
