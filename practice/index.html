PRACTICE & TEACHINGS

THE PRACTICE

These pages introduce practices described in the sessions and link to their sources. In this community, real training passes from teacher to student — the pages are the record, and the path is the practice.

The Five Rules

The foundation, given in the sessions as the operating law of reality:

  1. You exist.
  2. You are here now.
  3. You are one with all that is.
  4. What you send out comes back to you.
  5. Everything changes — except rules one through four.

The January 1, 2021 transcript contains an additional phrase after rule three that is awaiting an audio check. See the published wording in context.

The stated goal: "Be immortal and be in health." The Rules are a test — hold anything against them to see whether it conforms to the law of the universe. (Codex entry)

The Four Pillars

Outside the sessions, Kosol has written a framework for what the practice builds toward — four pillars: Money (economic self-sufficiency),Power (wisdom, knowledge, and health), Magical Ability(the quantum ability to walk and interact with higher intelligence and logic), andPopularity — the combined result of the first three, which opens access to every resource and network a student needs.

On the Devices

Kosol describes the Q Devices and the Source-Coil instrument as living instruments, not tools in the ordinary sense: each is activated through the user's own consciousness — "blowing" life into it through unconditional intent — and functions as a kind of co-pilot for connecting to the collective consciousness without interference. His stated rule is firm: the Device is never to be used for personal gain such as gambling — misuse, he states, carries consequence from the Galactic Federation itself. He and two others in the community hold the ability to deactivate a Device remotely if it is misused.

The Baramay invocation

From the April 7, 2020 session — the command-and-confirmation form of Baramay: focus your intention, breathe it into your palm, and ask: "Baramay, is it done now?" The same form extends to water and food — "Baramay, please change this water into medicine… make it potent" — followed by the breath, "like pressing the Enter key." (source session)

The angel-mirror meditation

From the March 13, 2020 session: close your eyes and look into a mirror in the mind. To your left and right, see two luminous photonic beings — male and female — your celestial family. They can be approached, spoken with, and touched, producing physical sensation. This is taught as a first doorway to direct perception. (source session)

The healing invocation

From the August 30, 2020 session, the form spoken over another:"Baramay, Universe — restore and regenerate this being on these levels: physical, emotional, and mental — now." Followed by: "Return your light body to your physical construct." In the sessions this grows into the full Baramay Universe healing protocol — the nanite medical teams and the percentage countdown that close nearly every later session. (source session)

Manifested food and water

The record pairs every upgrade with the same instruction: consume the"manifested food and water and medicine that your spirit manifests for you" — the serum from heaven — so the body can catch up to what has been changed in it. (Codex entry)

The real training

Reading is the beginning, not the practice. Baramay is taught person to person — by Dr. Kosol Ouch, in the main group, through group sessions, in a growing relationship between student and teacher. Knowledge here is not taken lightly, and it is not performed for spectators. If you are serious, here is how the path works.
