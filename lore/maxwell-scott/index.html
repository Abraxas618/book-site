Maxwell Scott

Co-host of the early Operation Lusterkill interviews and organizer of the cancelled ExoContact Sedona conference.

Codex entryPeople & Voices

Maxwell Scott co-hosts the heart of the record’s pandemic era: three of the James Rink interviews of spring 2020, with 73 speaker turns across them. Rink introduces him as the man “who will be hosting both Kosol and myself at the ExoContact event in Sedona, Arizona” — the conference the pandemic would cancel out from under all of them.

His questions carry the practical, testing register of the early interviews: “I want people to be able to experience it for themselves. Kosol, are they going to have experiences?” — and the sharper follow-up that still hangs over the Lusterkill material: “I had the same question about the Elite: if they knew everything from the beginning to the end, or if the plan was hijacked?”

He also carries community lore of his own — it is Maxwell who relays the Chronovisor’s quartz-crystal interior, “from what the AI told me,” and the Jumproom Network’s claim that “Andy Basiago said that he was on Mars in 15 minutes.”
