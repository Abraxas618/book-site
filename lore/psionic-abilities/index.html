Psionic & Psychic Abilities

The reawakening powers — fifteen sessions of psionic protocols, enhancements, and the claim that the abilities were always there.

Codex entryTeachings & Practice

Psionic abilities are the record’s promise to the individual: the latent powers — telepathy, clairvoyance, remote perception, manifestation — that the sessions treat not as gifts to be granted but as functions to be switched back on. Fifteen sessions carry the vocabulary from 2020 through 2025.

The teaching pairs the powers with their activation method: “Believe in yourself. Practice. Psionic ability protocol from the entity Kosol” (April 2020) — the belief protocol as ignition. The upgrades administered in-session name them as targets: “Enhancement of your psionic functions. Brain activity enhancement of (your) Cakra & Neu[ronet]…” And the forecast globalizes them: “psychic development, psionic abilities, will be enhanced within all of your cultural systems” — the coming era, in the record’s telling, is a psychic one by default.

The community’s own training (the super soldier curriculum, the meditation practice, the Q Devices) is the delivery system for exactly this: the archive’s practice stack exists to make psionics ordinary.
