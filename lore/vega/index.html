Vega

A star system the archive names as a cradle of humanity's evolving lineage.

Codex entryPlaces & Phenomena

Vega — a star in the Lyra constellation — appears in the archive as one of the cradles of humanity’s lineage. In the August 22, 2020 Insectoid session, Unimetrix 1 tells a questioner “your species is not here yet — still in the Vega star system, still evolving into sentient level,” and in the Bigfoot session it speaks of “your ancestor in the Vega star system.”

Vega is closely tied to the Lyrans and the Sirians in the archive’s genealogy — part of the ancestral star map from which Unimetrix 1 says humanity and its neighbours descend.

As a place, Vega marks one of the archive’s origin points: a home system in the deep past of the long story Unimetrix 1 tells about where humanity comes from.
