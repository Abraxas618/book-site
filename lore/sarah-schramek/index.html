Sarah Schramek

The questioner healed by 98,000 nanites in the June 2020 session — and a voice in the Unit X14 dialogue on pain and suffering.

Codex entryPeople & Voices

Sarah Schramek speaks in three sessions of 2020, and receives one of the record’s most detailed on-air healings. In the June 23 session — the “E.T. PHONE HOME” session with Ori — her simple request (“Can you scan my body and remove any viruses that are negative?”) triggers the full protocol: Nano-Medical Nanites “from our timeline” initiated by the tens of thousands, injected, and narrated to completion — the passage that became the archive’s canonical example of the healing practice.

She returns in the Lebanon attack session that August, and in October brings one of the record’s most human questions to Unit X14, the instructor unit on pain: “What about pain and suffering that is caused in a personal relationship, and that maybe has damaged somebody?” — grounding the record’s grand machinery in the size of an ordinary hurt.
