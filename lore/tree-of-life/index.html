Tree of Life

A 2020 book passage interprets branches, pearls, roots, and the sphere as symbols in a cosmic map.

Codex entryConcepts

The Tree of Life appears in the 2020 second edition as a “map to all reality.” The passage reads the diagram symbolically:

  • Its ten branches represent the binary digits 1 and 0.
  • Its seven blue pearls represent the seven days of a cosmic cycle.
  • Its eight roots, added to the ten branches, make 18; the digits reduce to 9, which the book associates with completion, 360 degrees, the sphere, and equality.

The book connects this symbolism with the Q-Devices, the Protomolecule, Unimetrix, cryptocurrency, and the Light Body. It presents them as reflections of spiritual creativity and of humanity’s connection with universal consciousness.

This page summarizes the book’s interpretation; it is not a scientific account of mathematics or cosmology. (Unimetrix1, Second Edition, E-BookTime, October 2020, pp. 553–554.)
