The Moon

Not a moon — a planetary quantum computer housing the Anunnaki collective consciousness.

Codex entryPlaces & Phenomena

“This moon that you call moon is not a moon.” In the December 7, 2020 session, the Anunnaki teacher — speaking as the collective creator “Azocar” — declares the Moon a planetary quantum computer: an artificial body housing the Anunnaki collective consciousness, from which it says it watches Earth (“I reside in the sky… I look toward you”).

The sessions extend the idea into a program: the Moon as one node of a solar-system-wide computer network being assembled — kin to the archive’s other great machine minds, from Unimetrix 1’s own planet-sized future computers to the crystal outpost at Pulemelei Mound. The Bigfoot session adds inhabitants: the space-faring “blue tribe” living on the Moon and Mars.

The interview sessions add a second origin: the Moon as a “Reptilian defense planetoid” — a fragment of the destroyed world whose debris forms the asteroid belt — towed to Earth by the Pleiadians “to balance the Earth’s environment.” Earth once had two moons, the same account says, and the fall of the other figures in the destruction of Atlantis. The archive preserves both tellings — Anunnaki vault and Pleiadian gift — as spoken.

An artificial Moon is an old motif of the hidden-history genre; the archive’s version gives it a precise function — the vault of humanity’s makers, orbiting the species that carries their genes.
