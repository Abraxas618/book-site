The Ley Lines & the Planetary Grid

The Earth's electromagnetic acupuncture system — power source for portals, highway for the Jumproom Network, symbolized by the Dragon — running through sixteen sessions.

Codex entryPlaces & Phenomena

The Ley Lines are the record’s infrastructure layer for the planet itself: an electromagnetic grid the sessions describe as the Earth’s own circulatory system, tapped by every civilization — human, secret, and otherwise — that has needed power or passage. Unimetrix 1 gives the cleanest definition in the March 26, 2020 session: the Ley-line structure is “a Planetary acupuncture point (system)” — and adds a decoding the record returns to repeatedly: “The Dragon symbolises the Ley-line structure, the grid-point system.” Where a dragon appears in myth or heraldry, the record reads a map of the grid.

The grid is first a power source. The Angkor Wat quantum computer is described as “opening portal using electromagnetic grid. What you call ley line as power source. Allow interaction with all parallel dimension.” Ancient super-chips “made of stone” are “powered by the electromagnetic grid-points that you Humans call the Ley-line Grid.” The recounted Atlantis ran its crystal generators on the same lines, and where major lines intersect, the sessions place “interdimensional portal or planetary chakra.”

It is also a road network. The secret space program’s Jumproom Network is said to “follow Space-time through the Ley-lines, the Galactic Ley-lines. And the off-world Ley-lines” — because in the record’s cosmology the grid does not stop at the atmosphere: stars connect through “transconduits, which are the Ley-lines… of different stars,” with the Sun serving as a stargate. The same session offers a folk etymology — ley as “Field or Meadow-Line” — for a system it treats as anything but pastoral.

Contested territory, naturally, is part of the picture: audiences ask whether hostile races are given “access to invade certain Ley-lines,” and the grid appears in the darker accounts of control structures as something that can be occupied, dammed, or tapped — the same wire that powers a healing temple powering something else.
