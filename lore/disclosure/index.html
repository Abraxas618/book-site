Disclosure

The managed revelation — ET reality released 'one step at a time' by different departments, with the sessions claiming to be part of the program: 'We are disclosure!'

Codex entryConcepts

Disclosure, in the record, is not an event awaiting a press conference — it is a program already running, with a schedule, departments, and (the sessions insist) this very archive on its roster. Nine sessions from 2020 to 2026 carry the doctrine.

The mechanism is gradualist: “Different departments will present different types of disclosure, to allow your consciousness, collectively, to process (it all) one step…” (May 2020) — revelation dosed like medicine, ahead of First Contact. And when questioners complain that no proof ever arrives, the record’s answer is its boldest recursion: “Disclosure is nonevidential in your perspective, (but) disclosure is happening… now, we are also disclosure! Do you understand, now? We are disclosure!” — the sessions presenting themselves as the program’s community-scale delivery channel.

By the June 2026 interrogation the framing is fully bureaucratic: “Data stream is rerouted to a different dimension for the purpose of disclosure and contact preparation, from an intergalactic alliance.” Whether one reads that as revelation or as an unfalsifiable loop, the record is at least consistent: the proof of the program is the telling of it.
