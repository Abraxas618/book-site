Eric Luny

The Canadian questioner of the July 2020 Earth Planetary Council session, whose questions draw out Unimetrix 1's statement of the two-way relationship between eras.

Codex entryPeople & Voices

Eric Luny introduces himself with the record’s plain courtesy — “Yes, UniMetrix, this is Eric Luny, from Canada” — and carries seventeen turns across the July 8, 2020 session that bears his name in its title: the negotiation with the Earth Planetary Council over Ori’s return home.

His questions in that session cut straight at the record’s geopolitics — “Greetings. Does China control the entire Planet?” — and one of them, Eric’s pressing (“I understand. Very interesting: so, the battle is not over…?”), draws out Unimetrix 1’s fullest statement of the two-way relationship between eras — “We are the future perspective of you, how you see yourself. You are the past perspective of us” — the passage the record’s whole stance rests on, answered to Eric Luny.
